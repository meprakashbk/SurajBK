Who I Am

The Player
Behind the Coach

A highly accomplished Nepali football player and coach with over 16 years of professional experience. Known for his commanding presence as a center defender, Suraj has represented Nepal at the national level and competed across AFC competitions in multiple countries.

With 23+ goals and 18+ assists from a defensive position across top Nepali clubs — and multiple Best Defender awards — Suraj brings elite-level tactical understanding to his coaching. He works with individual players at all skill levels and with teams preparing for competitive tournaments.
